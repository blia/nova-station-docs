## Format

Each devlog/channel post should have Ukrainian and English versions.

Posts should be readable outside the private development repository:

- explain the decision in product language;
- avoid raw internal scratch notes;
- mention why it matters;
- keep implementation details concrete but not overwhelming;
- keep each language version publishable on its own.
